\section{Annotation-key provenance}
\label{ssec:annotation}

The evaluation keys used by the main results were authored by a single
reviewer who is one of the authors and is not blind to the hypothesis, and
the paper says so wherever it matters. The key is itself a measurement with
error; the main text reports the one recorded key disagreement rather than
settling it. No independent second annotation is claimed for any result in
this paper.

A blinded independent-review instrument accompanies the submission: a
self-contained review sheet presenting the 12 authored relation questions
over the same frozen packet frames the blinded model received --- with no
system outputs, no arm names, and no statement of the hypothesis --- along
with deterministic ingest and per-field agreement tooling (answerability,
ambiguity, referent selection, relation label; Cohen's $\kappa$ only where a
field's categories are finite and varied enough for chance correction to be
meaningful; exact agreement elsewhere). The sheet covers the 10 items
eligible for the primary evaluation and the 2 excluded items, retained so
that the exclusion judgement itself can be measured for reproducibility
rather than assumed. Any annotation collected with this instrument is
stored as evaluation-key reproducibility evidence and calibration data;
keys are never altered in response to it for results already scored.
