\section{Independent annotation}
\label{ssec:annotation}

The evaluation keys used by the main results were authored by a single
non-blind reviewer, and the paper says so wherever it matters. To measure
how reproducible those keys are, a \textbf{second annotator} --- blinded to
all system outputs, to the arm names, and to the hypothesis --- independently
reviewed the 12 authored relation questions: the 10 eligible for the primary
evaluation, and the 2 excluded items, retained deliberately so that the
\emph{exclusion} judgement itself (is the item ambiguous?) is measured for
reproducibility rather than assumed.

Agreement is reported per field as exact agreement --- answerability,
ambiguity, referent selection, and relation label --- with Cohen's $\kappa$
reported only where the field's categories are finite and varied enough for
chance correction to be meaningful; where one category dominates or the
field is free text, $\kappa$ is degenerate and only exact agreement is
reported. The second annotator's returned review is stored as
evaluation-key reproducibility evidence and future calibration data; it was
not used to retune the evaluated system, and no key was altered in response
to it for any result in this paper.
