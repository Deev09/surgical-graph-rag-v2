\section{Replica scorecards, per scene}
\label{ssec:replica}

The frozen Phase-8 Replica scorecards ship in the evidence pack as
\texttt{phase8\_scorecard\_replica\_office\_0.json},
\texttt{\ldots room\_0.json}, \texttt{\ldots room\_1.json} and
\texttt{\ldots room\_2.json}, plus their aggregate. They enter the
evaluation as a schema and outcome transfer only: the evaluator re-reads the
packed, already-scored outcomes, and every internal stage the packed
scorecard cannot support is recorded as \texttt{unknown}: delivery,
relation correctness and serialization are never inferred from the final
answer. \Cref{tab:replica} gives the per-scene outcome counts in the
scorecards' own category vocabulary, verified against the packed files.

\paragraph{The normalized outcome matrix.} The raw categories map to the
evaluator's outcome matrix, keyed by (expected outcome, result), via each
question's per-item record, never by the category name alone. A
\texttt{miss} whose record shows an explicit deferral maps to
(answer,\,abstain); a \texttt{miss} that answered incorrectly, incompletely
or empty-without-deferring maps to (answer,\,wrong); a
\texttt{false\_answer} splits on the question's expected outcome. Over the
56 items: (answer,\,correct) 4, (answer,\,wrong) 20, (answer,\,abstain) 4,
(empty,\,correct) 27, (empty,\,wrong) 1. Positives are never pooled with
true-empties, and no single ``accuracy'' is exposed: 27 of the 56 items
expect an empty answer, and pooling would let true-empties mask the
positive-row failures.

\paragraph{Membership-only rows carry no precision or recall.} The
\texttt{NEAR\_SURFACE} rows of these keys are membership-only and
non-exhaustive: the key asserts that listed pairs are near a surface, not
that unlisted pairs are not. Per the scorecards' own scope note, precision,
recall and F1 are computed only over exhaustive answer rows of
human-verified keys; on non-exhaustive rows the evaluator \emph{refuses} to
emit precision, recall or true-negative metrics, and those rows enter only
as membership pass/fail.
