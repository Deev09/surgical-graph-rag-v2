\begin{abstract}
End-to-end accuracy for 3D scene-graph question answering obscures where
answerable questions are lost and whether component measurements support
deployable claims. We introduce StageReach3D, a scope-aware evaluator that
declares each evaluated arm's route through a fixed stage DAG, derives
reachability from gating dependencies, reports reached, pass, fail, and
unknown separately, and prevents deployable traces from consuming oracle-fed
answer-path stages. Evaluator-masked fault injection localizes \SRFaultLocalized{}
controlled faults spanning \SRFaultClasses{} fault classes and
\SRFaultRelationTypes{} relation types to the mutated stage. On an
ARKitScenes handheld RGB-D slice, a paired substitution implicates identity
access: the same stored relations answer 7 of 10 scored questions with
human-supplied identity and 0 of 10 with the pipeline's labels; the
delivered-label arm's entity naming, question-to-label matching and referent
grounding are not separated, so the loss is not localized among them.
Stored-edge replay agrees with recomputation on all 12 authored items under
the shared NEAR convention, a serialization-consistency result only. An
oracle-free grounding bridge recovers 2 of 10 and fails all predeclared
gates; a single-pass direct-RGB comparator is the strongest deployable arm on the
development rooms yet fails both gates on a blinded one-scene pilot, so no transfer
claim is made. The evaluator is intended for any staged 3D-QA
pipeline; its validation is narrower: one fully observable pipeline, a fault
battery whose injectors and checkers share the evaluator's ontology, and a
Replica re-scoring demonstrating outcome-schema compatibility only.
StageReach3D turns a pooled score into inspectable, scope-bounded failure
attribution; all findings remain instance-bound.
\end{abstract}
