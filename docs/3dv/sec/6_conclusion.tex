\section{Conclusion}

A representation can hold information sufficient to answer questions that the deployable
query paths we instantiated do not reach. We measured that gap directly: the same stored
relations answer 7 of 10 with human-supplied identity and 0 of 10 with the identity the
system actually produces \rid{F35, F40}, while the serialized edges reproduce recomputed
geometry on all 12 tested items \rid{F63}. Identity grounding is the dominant measured
loss on this \texttt{NEAR} slice. Nothing here clears relation \emph{extraction}: no
additional loss was measured during serialization relative to recomputation under the
same convention, on twelve items, over human-resolved delivered objects, and semantic
correctness of the stored relations was not independently established.

The contributions, restated:
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep2pt \parskip0pt
  \item \textbf{The tool.} StageReach3D: per-arm paths declared as data over a fixed
  stage vocabulary, reachability derived from declared gating dependencies, reached,
  pass, fail and unknown reported separately, scope enforced in code, and outcome-keyed
  metrics that refuse pooled accuracy --- demonstrated on ARKitScenes per-arm
  reachability and on Replica schema and outcome transfer \rid{G08}.
  \item \textbf{The controlled validation.} \SRFaultLocalized{} artifact-level injected
  faults, spanning \SRFaultClasses{} fault classes and \SRFaultRelationTypes{} relation
  types, localized to the mutated stage while the evaluator was masked to the injected
  class, with zero false attributions on clean artifacts \rid{G10}.
  \item \textbf{The findings.} Real RGB substantially improves the label stage on
  oracle-matched instances \rid{C01, C02, C03, C04} --- a component evaluation on an
  oracle-selected denominator, \textbf{not end-to-end or deployable performance} ---
  with a control that supports, but does not prove, the object-texture reading
  \rid{C17, C18}. Stored relations hold answers under human-resolved identity that
  deployable grounding does not reach --- 7/10 \rid{F35} against 0/10 \rid{F40} and
  2/10 \rid{F45}. Direct RGB is the strongest measured path on the two development
  rooms --- 7/10 at 0.90 coverage \rid{F50, F53}, wrong on 2 of 10 there \rid{F51} ---
  under one named model (claude-opus-5), one frozen pass; on the pilot room it answered
  5 of 10 with zero wrong answers \rid{F76, F77}, declined every non-co-visible item
  \rid{F79}, and failed both predeclared gates \rid{F82, F83}, so no cross-room claim
  is made.
\end{enumerate}

We do \textbf{not} claim that representations in general hold unreachable information,
that \emph{no} deployable path could reach it, or any ordering among 7, 2 and 0. Every
number is bound to this pipeline, these rooms, these arms and one frozen pass; what
transfers is the evaluator --- its schema, its gating semantics and its protocol. Every
negative result stands as measured; none was retuned or re-run.
