\section{Evidence pack and reproducibility}
\label{ssec:pack}

Every load-bearing number traces to the evidence pack that accompanies the
submission: \textbf{23 sanitized numeric reports} --- the 18 reports of the
original pack plus the 5 frozen Replica scorecards added for this submission
--- together with a manifest (\texttt{MANIFEST.json}) recording, for each
report, its original path in the authors' tree, the SHA-256 of both the
original and the sanitized bytes, and the producing commit. The sanitization
policy (bulk per-element keys dropped, oversized lists elided) is applied by
a dedicated packing tool that never regenerates a report: what is packed is
byte-derived from what was scored.

Twenty-eight of the 278 registry rows cite an untracked primary source and
cannot be reproduced from the packaged artifacts alone; each such row says
so in its source column. A further 52 quantities could not be verified
against any committed artifact and are enumerated in the accompanying
results census rather than entered in the registry at all. None of these is
load-bearing for any claim in the main paper's results section.
